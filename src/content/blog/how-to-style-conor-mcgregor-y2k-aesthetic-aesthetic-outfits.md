To truly encapsulate the Y2K vibe with your Conor McGregor The Notorious shirt, accessories are paramount. The early 2000s were all about making a statement with your extras.

*   **Tinted Sunglasses:** Small, oval, or rimless sunglasses in vibrant colors like orange, blue, or pink are essential.
